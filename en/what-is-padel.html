What is padel?

Padel is a racket sport played in doubles on a small glass-walled court. It is far easier to pick up than tennis: you will have real rallies within your first hour. That is exactly why it became the fastest-growing sport in the world.

How is it played?

The court is 10 by 20 metres, enclosed in glass — a ball bouncing off the wall stays in play, which makes rallies longer and more fun.

Always two versus two. Serves are underarm — unlike tennis, you will master them in a minute.

Scoring is the same as tennis: 15-30-40-game. If you have ever watched tennis, you already know the rules.

Do I need equipment?

No. Padel rackets are stringless and perforated; balls look like tennis balls with slightly less pressure. At V7, rackets and balls are waiting on court — just bring trainers.

Who is it for?

Everyone from 7 to 70. The court is small, so there is little running — reflexes and fun matter more than fitness. Most of our Antalya players started from zero.

Is padel hard to learn?

No — it is the fastest racket sport to pick up. Rallies on day one, confident play within weeks. Tennis takes 6-12 months to feel good.

Is padel the same as tennis?

No. Smaller court, underarm serve, walls in play, and always doubles. Only the scoring is shared.
