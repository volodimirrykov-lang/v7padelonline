FAQ

Questions you may have

01Is the 1,000₺ an entry price?

No. It confirms your attendance and remains your money inside V7PADEL. Use it later for a game, training or another available club service.

02Can I attend only as a spectator? Do I still confirm?

Yes, you can enjoy the music, celebration and support players without going on court. Invitations are limited, so every guest confirms attendance in the same way.

03What should I wear?

The dress code is sport / padel. If you plan to play, bring full sportswear and court shoes. If you are only watching, a sporty look is enough.

04Do I need to register for court activities in advance?

No. Decide at the celebration and easily join a court in turn.

05How do I get a lottery ticket?

From 24 August, join any tournament, open game or training during Birthday Week and receive one guaranteed ticket.

06What is V7 Balance?

It is your money inside V7PADEL. Use it later for a game, training or another available club service.

07Can I come with kids?

Yes. Mention children when confirming so we can prepare the family area.

08When is the main evening?

Friday, 28 August, from 17:00 until a warm close around 21:00.
